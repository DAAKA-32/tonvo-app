## First-72h kill rules

### Ad #1 — Count to 7
- **Hold-rate <30% at 3s** → recut the open. The dead-audio gap at 00:06–00:10 is the scroll-stop; if hold dies before then, the chrome 7-second hero at 00:00–00:02 isn't earning the lean-in. Swap to hook #4.5 ("Count to 7. Don't blink.") on the same body.
- **CTR <0.8% with hold >40%** → body lands but CTA fold is sleepy. Recut last 3s with the friction-killer triad from `#swipe-07` (Fathom): `Free starter / 30s setup / catches the close on call 1.`
- **CPS >$80 at 48h with both above healthy** → pull. Audience mismatch, not creative; route to media-buyer to reseat in ICP-2 ad set.

### Ad #2 — The Line That Closes
- **Hold-rate <35% at 5s** → the 3-stacked-objection beat at 00:02–00:06 is too dense. Recut to single objection ("Send me pricing") sustained 5s with mirror rebuttal landing at 00:05.
- **CTR <1.0% with hold >50%** → the demo capture at 00:11–00:16 isn't reading as live product. Tighten the iPhone 17 mockup zoom and slow the glass card surface from 0.3s to 0.6s so the rebuttal text is fully readable.
- **CPS >$70 at 72h with both above healthy** → pull. The relief register isn't converting cold; reserve for retargeting ad set only.

### Ad #3 — The Signal You Missed
- **Hold-rate <32% at 3s** → ICP-2 isn't claiming the hook. Swap to hook #3.2 ("70% of reps miss this line.") — pure stat-cold-open beats the loss-aversion cold-open on pride audiences.
- **CTR <0.9% with hold >45%** → the 9-vs-1 tile split at 00:10–00:14 is reading abstract. Replace with literal Zoom-thumbnail grid where 9 tiles say "kept pitching" and 1 says "asked for the deal."
- **CPS >$75 at 72h with both above healthy** → pull and re-cut for retargeting only. ICP-2 cold acquisition wants metric-led (Angle B in `copy.md`); this angle becomes a warm-audience reinforcer.

### Ad #4 — You Never Asked
- **Hold-rate <40% at 5s** → the autopsy frame is the strongest hook in the bank on paper but the highest-shame; some ICP-1 viewers will deflect. Recut with hook #1.3 ("Great demo. You forgot to close.") — validation-then-pull softens the entry without losing the punch.
- **CTR <1.1% with hold >50%** → the timeline-rewind beat at 00:19 isn't earning the close. Lengthen the `MIN 32 · ASKED · CLOSED · $61K` reveal from 3s to 4s and add a chrome silver sweep on the dollar amount.
- **CPS >$70 at 72h with both above healthy** → pull only if Ad #1 is converting; otherwise hold — #4 carries the campaign's signature numbers (47 / 8,412 / 0) and is the most distinctive creative even if not the most efficient.
