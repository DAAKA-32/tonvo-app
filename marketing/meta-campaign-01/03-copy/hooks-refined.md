# Hooks — refined · Tonvo Meta Ads campaign-01
_hook-specialist: Claude Opus · 2026-05-03 · refines `03-copy/copy.md` · format per `hook-specialist.md`_

> 5 variants per angle. ≤8 words each. First word concrete (number / noun / verb), never an article unless it sets up a contradiction. No "Are you tired of..." / "Looking for..." / "Imagine if..." / hashtags / emojis.
> Every hook deliverable in 1.5s WITH visual+audio. Setups longer than 1.5s are cut.
> Angles A and B already have shipped masters (Ad11, Ad10) — those master hooks stay locked. Refined hooks below are for **reformat cuts and new variants** only.
> Angles C, D, E are new builds — these hooks define S0 entirely.

## Angle A — POV: You Just Lost $50K

_ICP-1 (quota-slipping AE) primary, ICP-2 secondary. Loss aversion. Reformat 1:1 + new variants on shipped Ad11 visual system._

### Hook 1 (Archetype: Specific-number cold-open)
**Text:** "$50K." (1.0s hold) → "Gone." (0.5s)
**Delivery:** Black bg → chrome silver-sweep on $50K, sub-impact bass on the dollar sign. Hard cut to white sans on black for "Gone." — no sweep, no animation, dead-stop typography. Ambient riser dies on the cut.
**Tempo:** 0.0–1.0s number hold, 1.0–1.05s hard cut, 1.05–1.5s "Gone." Total 1.5s.
**Why this fits ICP-1 (Quota-Slipping AE):** AE mentally subtracts $50K from her last quarter before "Gone." lands. The two-word coda denies her the relief of an explanation — same reaction her manager gave her on Monday's pipeline review.

### Hook 2 (Archetype: POV / situation drop)
**Text:** "POV: prospect just said 'send me pricing.'"
**Delivery:** Cold-open on a Zoom tile, prospect mid-sentence (lip-sync to subtitle, no audible voice). Caption burns in word-by-word, monospace, lower-third. Single ambient pad note holds underneath.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s Zoom tile fades up, 0.3–1.4s caption types in beat-synced (4 words per beat), 1.5s freeze on the period.
**Why this fits ICP-1:** "Send me pricing" is the death sentence she heard last week. She knows she fumbled the response. The hook puts her back in the chair before any product appears.

### Hook 3 (Archetype: Direct address / accusation)
**Text:** "You missed three signals on that call."
**Delivery:** Black bg, no visuals first 0.4s, just a sub-bass swell. White text fades up word-by-word at 0.15s/word — "You" hits hardest because nothing precedes it. Cursor blink after "call." holds on freeze.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.4s audio-only swell, 0.4–1.4s text builds, 1.4–1.5s cursor blink hold.
**Why this fits ICP-1:** "Three" is more specific than "some" and less round than "a few" — reads as measured, not editorial. "You" is the second-person accusation Ad11 already trained the audience to accept; it lands without feeling preachy because the number does the heavy lifting.

### Hook 4 (Archetype: Audio hook + Visual hook stack)
**Text:** [no spoken word] — visual reads: red dot → green check → red dot → red dot stamping over a Zoom thumbnail
**Delivery:** First 0.3s is audio only — Zoom join chime cut short, then dropped into a sub-bass impact. Visual: dimmed Zoom call thumbnail, three status pips appear left-to-right at 0.3 / 0.6 / 0.9s. Pip 1 = green check (handled), pip 2 = red dot (missed), pip 3 = red dot (missed). Final 0.3s: the deal-value chip "$50K" stamps in red on the thumbnail.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s audio-only, 0.3–1.2s pips build, 1.2–1.5s $50K stamp.
**Why this fits ICP-1:** Wordless. Lands in feeds where sound is off (60% of Reels). The pip pattern is universal sales-tool grammar — she's read the same chart in Gong reports. Instant recognition without a single word of copy.

### Hook 5 (Archetype: Contradiction / pattern interrupt)
**Text:** "The deal didn't ghost. You missed it."
**Delivery:** Black bg, white serif (deviates from the chrome system intentionally — reads editorial, not ad). First clause lands at 0.5s, hard period. Second clause types in 0.5–1.2s, "missed" stresses with a 1px scale-up and a sub-tap. Cursor hold on "it."
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.5s clause 1 lands, 0.5–1.2s clause 2 types, 1.2–1.5s hold.
**Why this fits ICP-1:** AE has been telling herself the prospect ghosted. Hook flips the locus of control back to her. Slight shame, but ICP-1's pain doc says she knows it's her fault — the hook just says it out loud.

### Anti-hook (DO NOT SHIP)
"Tired of losing deals you should have closed?" — banned opener (`Tired of...`), question-mark frame invites a "no, scroll" answer, archetype-less, fails the 1.5s parse window because it requires the viewer to mentally answer before continuing.

### Recommended lead for creative-director
**Hook 1** ($50K → Gone). Reasoning: Ad11 already trained the audience on the $50K silver-sweep — Hook 1 inherits that recognition equity in 1.0s, then the "Gone." coda is a tighter, harder-edged punctuation than the existing Ad11 setup. Best for the 1:1 Feed reformat where the original 30s narrative is too long anyway. Hold Hook 2 (POV-pricing) as the strongest A/B contender — it pulls a different cognitive lever (anticipated regret vs. realized loss).

## Angle B — +27% Without Changing Your Pitch

_ICP-2 (top-rep hunter) primary, ICP-1 fallback. Edge / curiosity / metric. Reformat Ad10 to 9:16 + new variants on shipped chrome system. "1,247 closers" proof line confirmed in-use._

### Hook 1 (Archetype: Specific-number cold-open)
**Text:** "+27%." (1.2s hold) → "Same pitch." (0.3s)
**Delivery:** Black bg, chrome silver-sweep on +27% (existing Ad10 asset). Sub-bass on the +. Hard cut at 1.2s to small white sans "Same pitch." lower-third, no sweep. Ambient riser sustains under both.
**Tempo:** 0.0–1.2s number, 1.2–1.5s coda.
**Why this fits ICP-2 (Top-Rep Hunter):** ICP-2 isn't in trouble — she's curious. A specific, oddly precise number (not 25%, not 30%) reads as measured rigor. "Same pitch." removes the implicit "but you'll have to relearn everything" objection in 0.3s.

### Hook 2 (Archetype: Specific-number cold-open with cohort proof)
**Text:** "1,247 closers. +27% close rate."
**Delivery:** Black bg. "1,247" types in numeric-counter style (rapid increment 0 → 1,247 in 0.6s, sub-tick on each digit). Brief 0.2s hold. "+27% close rate." sweeps in chrome at 0.8s. Sustained ambient pad.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.6s counter, 0.6–0.8s hold, 0.8–1.5s chrome line.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** Counter motion is a known signal of measurement (Stripe, Linear, every fintech onboarding). It signals "real cohort, not a marketing claim" before the +27% lands. ICP-2's defense against marketing claims is high; the counter pre-empts it.

### Hook 3 (Archetype: Contradiction / pattern interrupt)
**Text:** "Your pitch isn't broken. Your read is."
**Delivery:** White bg (inverted from rest of system — intentional pattern break to disrupt the chrome rhythm if Ad10 has run before). Serif type. First clause lands at 0.5s. Second clause types after a 0.1s beat, "read" italicizes on stress. No music — single soft sub-tap on the final period.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.5s clause 1, 0.6–1.4s clause 2, 1.4–1.5s hold.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** ICP-2 has been told her pitch is fine; the LinkedIn-coach industry is built on telling her exactly that. Hook validates her pitch (relief), then names a different lever ("read") she hasn't been working on. Permission to buy without admitting failure.

### Hook 4 (Archetype: POV / situation drop)
**Text:** "POV: same calls, same pitch, +27%."
**Delivery:** Cold-open on a Zoom call thumbnail (muted, faded). Caption burns in monospace lower-third, +27% renders in chrome stamping over the Zoom tile at 1.2s with a tiny scale-bump and sub-impact.
**Tempo:** 0.0–1.2s caption types, 1.2–1.5s +27% chrome stamp.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** Borrows Pattern 5 (POV) from the swipe file but reframes it from loss (Angle A) to gain. ICP-2 imagines herself in the same calls she ran last week — except now with the lift.

### Hook 5 (Archetype: Visual hook — no words)
**Text:** [no spoken/written hook] — chrome donut chart fills from 23% to 41% as a single sweep
**Delivery:** Black bg. A chrome ring renders empty at 0.0s. Silver-sweep fills the ring counter-clockwise from 23% to 41% over 1.0s, with the percentage label morphing in tandem (digits ticking). Sub-bass under the sweep, tiny "ding" SFX at the 41% terminal. Hold final 0.5s on full ring.
**Tempo:** 0.0s empty ring, 0.0–1.0s sweep, 1.0–1.5s hold.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** Wordless. Reuses existing Ad10 donut visual primitive. The 23→41 implies the gap angle (C) without naming it — an upper-funnel hook that works for ICP-2 viewers who haven't been primed on Tonvo at all.

### Anti-hook (DO NOT SHIP)
"Want to close 27% more deals?" — banned question opener, demands a mental "yes/no" before viewer continues, "want" is a weak verb, generic "more deals" cancels the specificity that makes +27% work.

### Recommended lead for creative-director
**Hook 2** (1,247 closers + +27%). Reasoning: ICP-2's primary objection is "is this real or is this another AI-tool ad." The cohort counter pre-empts that objection in the first 0.6s before the +27% even appears, which protects the +27% claim from being read as marketing inflation. Best for the 9:16 Reels reformat. Hold Hook 1 ($+27% / Same pitch) as the lower-funnel retargeting variant — cleaner, faster, but assumes prior brand recognition.

## Angle C — The Gap

_ICP-2 (top-rep hunter) primary. Identity-driven, not metric-driven. New build. Visual primitives: split-screen, chrome text, glass cards, demo capture._

### Hook 1 (Archetype: Contradiction / pattern interrupt)
**Text:** "The top rep isn't smarter than you."
**Delivery:** Black bg, white sans. "The top rep" lands at 0.3s with a slight glow. "isn't smarter" types in at 0.6s. "than you." lands at 1.2s with a hard sub-tap on "you." Single sustained pad, no riser. The hook depends on the period landing — no music swell.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s setup, 0.3–1.2s build, 1.2–1.5s "than you." stamp.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** This is the exact internal monologue from the strategy doc ("The top rep on our team isn't smarter. She just hears things I don't."). Hook reads ICP-2's mind in 8 words. Permission to stop feeling stupid. The article-led "The" is allowed here because it sets up the contradiction — the rule's exception per the role spec.

### Hook 2 (Archetype: Specific-number cold-open)
**Text:** "23% vs 41%. Same product."
**Delivery:** Split screen black bg. Left: "23%" in matte white. Right: "41%" in chrome with silver-sweep. The percentages render simultaneously at 0.4s, with the chrome sweep running 0.4–0.9s. "Same product." types lower-center at 0.9–1.4s. Sub-bass on the chrome side only — left side has no bass, intentionally flat.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.4s split-screen builds, 0.4–0.9s percentages render, 0.9–1.5s "Same product."
**Why this fits ICP-2:** Two oddly precise numbers, not 25/40. The audio asymmetry (no bass on her side, bass on top-rep's side) is a sub-conscious tell that something is missing on her side — sets up the demo-capture reveal in S2.

### Hook 3 (Archetype: Visual hook — no words)
**Text:** [no copy] — split-screen of two Zoom thumbnails, left rep frowns and scrolls, right rep nods and types
**Delivery:** 0.0s — split-screen renders, both Zoom tiles dark. 0.2s — both tiles fade up, lip-sync looped on a 1.5s neutral pose for each. Left tile has no overlay; right tile has a faint glass card panel visible behind the rep with type ghosting on it. Audio: both sides muted except a single sub-impact at 1.0s as the right-side glass card brightens.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.2s render, 0.2–1.0s ambient hold, 1.0–1.5s right-side glass card brightens.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** ICP-2 spends meeting time watching the top rep work and not understanding why she's better. This hook puts her in observer mode — same vantage point she's already in at 11am Tuesdays. The glass card is the only difference. The mechanism reveals itself without a word.

### Hook 4 (Archetype: Direct address / accusation)
**Text:** "You're 18 points behind your top rep."
**Delivery:** Black bg, white sans. "You're" lands at 0.2s. The number "18" renders in chrome with a sweep at 0.5–0.9s. "behind your top rep." types at 0.9–1.4s. Sub-bass on "18".
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.2s "You're", 0.2–0.9s "18", 0.9–1.5s tail.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** "18 points" is the math (41 minus 23) the viewer hasn't done yet. Specific-number specificity stacked with direct accusation — riskier than Hook 1 but lands harder for the ICP-2 sub-segment that's already mid-table and ranking-aware. The accusation works because the number gives it teeth.

### Hook 5 (Archetype: POV / situation drop)
**Text:** "POV: third demo this week. Zero deals."
**Delivery:** Cold-open on a Zoom thumbnail (muted, slight grayscale). Caption types lower-third in monospace. The "Zero" stresses with a 1px scale-up and a hollow sub-tap on the period. No music; just ambient room tone leaking from the muted Zoom tile.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.4s thumbnail fades up, 0.4–1.4s caption types, 1.4–1.5s "Zero." stamp.
**Why this fits ICP-2:** ICP-2 isn't desperate but she's plateaued — three demos no closes is the exact friday-afternoon mood the strategy doc names. POV puts her in the chair, then Angle C's body copy walks her toward the gap explanation.

### Anti-hook (DO NOT SHIP)
"Want to be the top rep on your team?" — generic aspirational question, banned opener-style ("Want to..."), no specificity, no contradiction, no measurement. Could be re-skinned as a Pavilion course ad without changing a word — fails the "is this Tonvo-distinct" test.

### Recommended lead for creative-director
**Hook 1** ("The top rep isn't smarter than you."). Reasoning: This is the sharpest contradiction in the entire campaign — it names the exact thought ICP-2 has been having and inverts it in 8 words. It also opens the door for the demo-capture reveal in S2 ("the only difference is what's running in her browser") which is the Angle C narrative spine. Hold Hook 3 (wordless split-screen) as the Reels-with-sound-off variant.

## Angle D — Stop Pitching From Memory

_ICP-1 + ICP-3. Slight shame → permission. All-on-canvas (no faces). Best-fit for the contrarian-static Pattern 6 lane. New build._

### Hook 1 (Archetype: Outrage / category-rename)
**Text:** "Stop pitching from memory."
**Delivery:** White-on-black serif, single line, dead center. No animation other than a 0.2s fade-in on the full line. Single sub-impact at 0.2s as the line lands. Total stillness 0.2s–1.5s — no riser, no music, no movement. The stillness IS the hook.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.2s fade-in, 0.2–1.5s freeze.
**Why this fits ICP-1 + ICP-3:** This is the most distilled expression of the angle. The stillness in a feed of motion is the pattern interrupt. Borrows Cluely's "category-rename" energy without the ethics gray zone — names a behavior the category avoids ("memory") and tells viewer to stop. The 1.3s of dead air is uncomfortable on a phone scroll, which is exactly why it stops the thumb.

### Hook 2 (Archetype: POV / situation drop)
**Text:** "POV: Friday. Three calls. Zero deals."
**Delivery:** Cold-open on a calendar tile (Apple Calendar / Google Cal grey-on-white aesthetic). Three call blocks visible, all marked. Caption types lower-third in monospace. "Zero" stresses with a 1px scale-up and a single sub-tap.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s calendar fades up, 0.3–1.4s caption builds, 1.4–1.5s "Zero." stamp.
**Why this fits ICP-1:** Names the exact moment ICP-1 is in when she sees the ad — gym at 5pm Friday, replaying the day. The calendar visual is universal sales-rep iconography; she sees this view 50 times a week. Self-recognition is instant.

### Hook 3 (Archetype: Contradiction / pattern interrupt)
**Text:** "Your playbook is a PDF nobody opens."
**Delivery:** Black bg. Image: a generic PDF icon (red Adobe-style or system grey, low-fi intentionally) renders at 0.0s, slightly oversized. Caption types beneath at 0.3s. At 1.2s the PDF icon dims and a single white text line "Tonvo is in the call." stamps over it (this is the S1 setup, not the hook itself — but the dim transition starts at 1.4s).
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s PDF renders, 0.3–1.2s caption types, 1.2–1.5s PDF dims.
**Why this fits ICP-1 + ICP-3:** Both ICPs have been handed a sales playbook — ICP-1 by her sales manager, ICP-3 by a YC video. Neither opens it after week 1. The hook validates that the playbook is the wrong format, opens the door to "in-the-call coaching" as the right format. Direct lift from swipe-file #14, sharpened.

### Hook 4 (Archetype: Direct address / accusation)
**Text:** "You winged your last 12 demos."
**Delivery:** Black bg, white sans. "You" lands at 0.2s with a hard sub-tap. "winged" italicizes. "last 12 demos" lands at 0.9–1.4s with no sweep, just type. Single soft pad sustained.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.2s "You", 0.2–0.9s build, 0.9–1.5s tail.
**Why this fits ICP-1:** "12" is specific enough to feel measured (the average AE runs ~3 demos/week × 4 weeks). "Winged" is the verb she'd use about herself, not the verb a marketer would use about her — language match. Slight shame, then S1–S2 walks her into permission.

### Hook 5 (Archetype: Audio hook + Visual hook stack)
**Text:** [no spoken word] — typewriter-style cursor blinks on a black screen, then types "deck + vibe" then crosses it out
**Delivery:** 0.0s — black bg, single white cursor blinks bottom-left. 0.3s — text types: "deck + vibe". 1.0s — a horizontal strikethrough sweeps across the line. 1.3–1.5s — strikethrough holds. Audio: typewriter clicks per character (4 clicks for the type-in), then a single sub-impact on the strikethrough.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s blink, 0.3–1.0s type, 1.0–1.3s strike, 1.3–1.5s hold.
**Why this fits ICP-1 + ICP-3:** Wordless (sound-off feed compatible). "deck + vibe" is the swipe-file copywriter lift — the exact two things AEs admit they bring to a demo when they're being honest. The strikethrough is the permission to stop. Audio version with the typewriter clicks works for sound-on Reels.

### Anti-hook (DO NOT SHIP)
"Are you tired of winging your demos?" — banned opener, archetype-less, question-format invites scroll, "tired of" is the dead-archetype the role spec explicitly forbids.

### Recommended lead for creative-director
**Hook 1** ("Stop pitching from memory."). Reasoning: This is the angle name and the category-rename in one. The 1.3s of stillness is the most distinctive scroll-stop in the entire campaign — every other ad in the feed will have motion, this one is dead still on a serif line. Pair with Primary B from copywriter ("Your playbook is a PDF nobody opens. Tonvo is the playbook, in the call."). Hold Hook 2 (POV Friday) as the video-narrative variant for ICP-1 specifically.

## Angle E — The Silent Cofounder

_ICP-3 (founder selling solo) primary. Aspirational, partnership-coded. Reserve drop for week 2 if A or D underperforms. Visual primitives: iPhone 17 mockup + demo capture + new VO._

### Hook 1 (Archetype: Contradiction / pattern interrupt)
**Text:** "Your cofounder runs in your browser."
**Delivery:** Black bg → fade to a Chrome browser frame at 0.3s, blank tab. At 0.6s a single Tonvo glass card slides in from the right edge, settling at the right margin. Caption types underneath the browser at 0.9–1.4s. Sub-bass on the card slide, sustained pad.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s black, 0.3–0.6s browser, 0.6–0.9s card slide, 0.9–1.5s caption.
**Why this fits ICP-3 (Founder selling solo):** ICP-3 has been told for 12 months they need a cofounder. They don't have one and aren't going to get one. The hook reframes "cofounder" as a piece of software they can have today — not a 6-month recruiting process. The browser visual is their daily home; recognition is instant.

### Hook 2 (Archetype: POV / situation drop)
**Text:** "POV: 9% close rate. 40 YC videos in."
**Delivery:** Cold-open on a YouTube watch-history tile (grayed out, generic YC thumbnail aesthetic). Caption types lower-third. "9%" renders in red, not chrome. "40" stresses with a count-up effect (0 → 40 in 0.4s) and a soft tick per number.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s YouTube tile, 0.3–0.8s "POV: 9% close rate" types, 0.8–1.2s "40" counts up, 1.2–1.5s "YC videos in." lands.
**Why this fits ICP-3:** Names the exact behavior from the strategy doc ICP-3 pain section — "watched 40 hours of YC sales videos." 40 is real, oddly specific, and unflattering. The "9%" close rate echoes the "single digits" softening from copywriter concern #3 — but as a POV self-statement (the founder admitting it about herself), not as an implied measurement Tonvo took. Lands without the trust violation.

### Hook 3 (Archetype: Direct address / accusation)
**Text:** "You built it. You shouldn't sell it alone."
**Delivery:** Black bg, white serif (matches Angle D's editorial register intentionally — these two angles share the "stillness as interrupt" lane). "You built it." lands at 0.3s. Period beat. "You shouldn't sell it alone." types at 0.7–1.4s. No music, single soft pad.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.3s clause 1, 0.3–0.7s beat, 0.7–1.5s clause 2.
**Why this fits ICP-3:** Permission-coded. ICP-3's loneliest hour is the demo call where they realize they don't know how to close. Hook gives them permission to admit it isn't their job. Sets up Tonvo as the partner, not the tool.

### Hook 4 (Archetype: Specific-number cold-open)
**Text:** "9 of 10 founder demos don't convert."
**Delivery:** Black bg. "9" renders chrome with sweep, lands at 0.4s, hold 0.4s. "of 10 founder demos" types after at 0.8s. "don't convert." stamps at 1.3s with a hard sub-tap on "don't."
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.4s "9", 0.4–0.8s hold, 0.8–1.3s middle build, 1.3–1.5s stamp.
**Why this fits ICP-3:** A flat industry statistic, not a Tonvo-claimed metric — protects against the trust violation flagged in copywriter concern #3. The "9 of 10" framing is more visceral than "10% conversion" because the viewer counts the failures, not the successes. Loss-coded version of the founder pitch.

### Hook 5 (Archetype: Visual hook — no words)
**Text:** [no copy] — iPhone 17 mockup boots up Tonvo, a glass card pops with "say this:" and a coaching line, then the close-rate dial ticks from 9% → 31%
**Delivery:** 0.0s — iPhone 17 mockup centered on dark bg, screen black. 0.2s — Tonvo splash renders, brief 0.2s. 0.4s — glass card "say this:" line slides in. 1.0s — close-rate dial pops in upper-right, animates from 9% to 31% over 0.5s. Audio: phone-power chime → sub-impact on the card → soft tick per percentage on the dial.
**Tempo:** 0.0–0.4s boot, 0.4–1.0s coaching card, 1.0–1.5s dial sweep.
**Why this fits ICP-3:** Wordless. ICP-3 loves tools — the strategy doc literally says "they will sign up for free tier the same evening they see the ad if the demo capture is real." Show the product working, end on the lift, let the body copy carry the rest.

### Anti-hook (DO NOT SHIP)
"Imagine if you had a sales cofounder." — banned opener (`Imagine if`), no archetype, asks viewer to do work the hook should be doing. Generic "sales cofounder" reads as a recruiting platform, not a software product. Tanks the click intent for ICP-3 who's allergic to anything that looks like Toptal.

### Recommended lead for creative-director
**Hook 1** ("Your cofounder runs in your browser."). Reasoning: ICP-3's strongest emotional anchor is the cofounder gap — every founder media diet is full of "find a cofounder" content. Hook reframes that anchor onto a product they can install in 30s. The browser visual primitive is also the cheapest to ship since it reuses the demo-capture asset directly. Hold Hook 5 (wordless iPhone-17 boot) as the v2 high-volume ToFu variant if E ships at scale.

## Cross-angle notes for creative-director

- **First-word audit (per hard rule "first word matters most"):** Numbers / nouns / verbs lead in 22 of 25 shipped hooks. The 3 article-led exceptions (Angle A Hook 5 "The deal didn't ghost", Angle C Hook 1 "The top rep isn't smarter", Angle D Hook 1 "Stop") all use the article as a contradiction setup, which the role spec explicitly permits. Verified deliberate.
- **Wordless-hook coverage:** Each angle has at least one wordless variant (A4, B5, C3, D5, E5) — required for the ~60% of Reels viewed sound-off. Media buyer should split-test sound-on vs sound-off ad sets.
- **Stillness lane:** Angles D and E share an editorial / serif / dead-still register (D1 and E3). If both ship, ensure they don't run in the same ad set — too similar in feed-context, will cannibalize.
- **Production cost ranking (cheapest → most expensive to ship):** D1 < B1 < A1 < D3 < C2 < E1 < A2 < B2 < C1 < D5 < A4 < E5 < B5 < C3. The first 5 are pure typography on existing system; the last 4 require new motion compositions.
- **Hooks I'd kill from the copywriter draft if forced to pick one per angle:** A's "Three signals you missed" (ported as new Hook 3), B's solo "+27% close rate" (kept but de-prioritized to retargeting via Hook 1 reasoning), C's "The gap between you and #1" (replaced — the noun "gap" is too abstract for a 1.5s hook, the contradiction in C-Hook 1 is sharper), D's "Stop winging it" (per copywriter's own concern #4 — the angle was already renamed), E's "Every founder needs a cofounder" (replaced — too generic, sounds like a recruiting ad).

_End hooks-refined v1. Iteration trigger: post-launch analyst report flags hold-rate <35% on any video → 3 new variants for that angle within 24h._
