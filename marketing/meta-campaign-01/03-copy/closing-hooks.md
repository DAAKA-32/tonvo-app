# Closing-Hooks — Tonvo Meta Ads campaign-01 (Closing Sprint)
_hook-specialist: Claude Opus · 2026-05-14 · extends `03-copy/hooks-refined.md` · scope locked by `01-strategy/02-closing-sprint-brief.md`_

> 36 first-1.5s hooks engineered for the close moment only. ≤8 words each. First word concrete (number / noun / verb). No banned openers. No duplicates of the 25 hooks already shipped in `hooks-refined.md`.
> Distribution: 6 hooks × 6 close-moment subjects. ICP-1 primary (70%), ICP-2 secondary (30%). ICP-3/ICP-4 out of scope this sprint.
> Voice lock: second-person, specific numbers, sales-coach-in-a-hallway. No marketing voice.

## Subject 1 — The Unasked Close

_Rep talked 47 min, never asked. Tonvo flags the moment to ask. ICP-1 primary._

### Hook 1.1
- **Text:** "47 minutes. You never asked."
- **Visual:** Chrome stopwatch glass card frozen at 47:00, red dot blinking where the "ask" should have been.
- **Subject:** Unasked close
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** specific-number + accusation. The "47" is the oddly-precise call length; she's run that exact call.

### Hook 1.2
- **Text:** "You pitched for 47 minutes. No ask."
- **Visual:** Zoom call duration counter ticks 46:58 → 47:00, then a chrome "ASK?" stamp drops in red over the timer.
- **Subject:** Unasked close
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** pattern-interrupt — the missing action named, not the action taken.

### Hook 1.3
- **Text:** "Great demo. You forgot to close."
- **Visual:** Glass card "Demo: ✓" sweeps in, then a second glass card "Close: —" lands beneath it. Em-dash blinks.
- **Subject:** Unasked close
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1, ICP-2
- **Mechanic:** validation-then-pull. Removes shame, names the gap.

### Hook 1.4
- **Text:** "Your last 8 demos. Zero asks."
- **Visual:** 8 tiny Zoom thumbnails in a 4×2 grid, each gets a small red "no-ask" pip stamped in sequence over 0.8s.
- **Subject:** Unasked close
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** specific-count — 8 is real (2 weeks of demos), the visual makes the pattern undeniable.

### Hook 1.5
- **Text:** "You talked. You never asked."
- **Visual:** Audio waveform fills the screen, sustained — at 1.2s it flat-lines and a chrome "?" never appears beside it.
- **Subject:** Unasked close
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1, ICP-2
- **Mechanic:** silence-as-stop. The missing question mark is the punch.

### Hook 1.6
- **Text:** "Minute 47. The ask never came."
- **Visual:** Black bg, chrome "47:00" timestamp lands center, then dims as a single red cursor blinks where the ask line should be.
- **Subject:** Unasked close
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** numeric-surprise + curiosity. Sets up the body's "Tonvo would have flagged it at minute 32."

### Hook 2.6

## Subject 4 — Silence After the Ask

_Rep finally asks, buyer goes silent, rep talks past the close. Coach: hold the silence, count to 7. ICP-1 primary._

### Hook 4.1
- **Text:** "You asked. Then you kept talking."
- **Visual:** Audio waveform shows a clean ask, then a 0.5s flat-line of silence, then the waveform spikes back up — red "DON'T" stamps over the second spike.
- **Subject:** Silence after the ask
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** pattern-interrupt — names the exact failure mode in seven words.

### Hook 4.2
- **Text:** "7 seconds of silence wins deals."
- **Visual:** Chrome "7" lands large, holds, then a thin chrome bar fills 0 → 7 across the bottom over 1.0s. No audio under the bar — intentional dead air.
- **Subject:** Silence after the ask
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1, ICP-2
- **Mechanic:** silence-as-stop + specific-number. The dead audio in a feed of noise is the hook.

### Hook 4.3
- **Text:** "First to speak loses. It's you."
- **Visual:** Split-screen, both reps silent. Left side's lip moves at 1.1s, a red "LOST" stamp drops on left side at 1.3s.
- **Subject:** Silence after the ask
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** rule-then-accusation. The sales adage made personal.

### Hook 4.4
- **Text:** "Asked. Panicked. Talked past the yes."
- **Visual:** Four chrome dots render left-to-right at 0.2s intervals — "ASK" / "—" / "—" / "TALK." The middle two stay blank (the silence she should have held).
- **Subject:** Silence after the ask
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** stepwise-failure. Four beats, she lost on beat three.

### Hook 4.5
- **Text:** "Count to 7. Don't blink."
- **Visual:** Chrome counter ticks 1 → 7 over 1.0s, no audio, single sub-impact on "7." Cursor holds.
- **Subject:** Silence after the ask
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1, ICP-2
- **Mechanic:** direct-instruction + silence-as-stop. Coach in the hallway, literal.

### Hook 4.6
- **Text:** "Silence is the close. You broke it."
- **Visual:** Black bg, flat audio waveform sustains for 0.8s, then spikes once (her voice) — red strikethrough sweeps across the spike.
- **Subject:** Silence after the ask
- **ICP fit:** ICP-1
- **Mechanic:** reframe + accusation. Renames silence from awkward to weapon.

## Top-7 Cut (rank-ordered bets)

The seven I'd put real money behind. Copywriter (wave 2) should lead with these.

1. **Hook 4.2 — "7 seconds of silence wins deals."**
   _Defense:_ Owns Subject 4 and Subject 6 in one line — the dead audio in a feed of noise is the most distinctive scroll-stop in the bank, and the "7" is the campaign's signature number.

2. **Hook 2.2 — "'Send me pricing' = you already lost."**
   _Defense:_ Names the most common death-line in B2B sales and inverts what the AE thought was progress; the equals sign visual translates instantly without VO.

3. **Hook 1.1 — "47 minutes. You never asked."**
   _Defense:_ Specific-number cold-open + accusation in 5 words; the 47 reads as a real call length, not a template, and "never asked" is the unasked-close angle in two words.

4. **Hook 5.2 — "Buyer: 'too expensive.' You: 'too expensive?'"**
   _Defense:_ The hook IS the technique — the viewer learns the mirror in 1.5s before the body has to teach anything. Lowest-cost-to-comprehend hook in the bank.

5. **Hook 3.4 — "Buyer just said yes. You missed it."**
   _Defense:_ Loss-aversion ported into the buying-signal subject; the implicit "you walked past a yes" is the worst feeling in a sales career, which is exactly the lever Tonvo monetizes.

6. **Hook 6.4 — "You had 7 seconds. You used 47."**
   _Defense:_ Stacks both anchor numbers (7 and 47) from the bank into one contradiction; the viewer doesn't need the body copy to feel the delta.

7. **Hook 4.5 — "Count to 7. Don't blink."**
   _Defense:_ Sales-coach-in-a-hallway voice in its purest form — five words, two imperatives, zero marketing. The literal hallway-line distilled to ad copy.
