# Tonvo Meta Ads — Campaign 01 Strategy

_Strategist: Claude Opus · Owner: Emilien · Date: 2026-05-03 · Version: v1 (pre-launch)_

---

## TL;DR

We are not selling "AI for sales calls." That market is loud, expensive, and dominated by Gong/Chorus on the analytics side and a swarm of GPT wrappers on the live-coaching side. We are selling **a +27% close-rate edge that turns on inside Zoom in 30 seconds, with no card and no IT ticket**.

Campaign 01 is a single-hypothesis test: **individual-rep PLG signups**, not team rollouts. Closer-priced ($19/mo) buyers who self-serve. We test creative angles around _missed money_ and _the gap between you and the top rep_, both shot in the visual language we already shipped (Ad11 POV $50K, Ad10 +27%).

Primary KPI: **Cost per signup**, target $8–$15 in cold US traffic at v1 budget. Realistic, not promised.

## 1. ICP Segments

We picked four. Three are buyers, one is a tripwire (manager/team lead — useful for retargeting and Sales Team plan upsell, not for the cold prospecting test).

### ICP-1 — "The Quota-Slipping AE" (primary, ~60% of v1 spend)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Account Executive (AE) — Mid-Market or SMB segment |
| **Company** | Series A–B SaaS, 10–150 employees, 5–20 reps on the floor |
| **Comp** | $70k base / $140k OTE, paid on closed-won not pipeline |
| **Pain trigger** | Just missed Q1 number. Tomorrow's pipeline review will be uncomfortable. Manager already mentioned a PIP in passing. |
| **Already tried** | Gong (company-paid, but it only tells them what went wrong _after_), GPT-4 with screenshare prompts, hand-written objection cards taped next to the laptop, $200/mo coaching from a LinkedIn "sales guru" |
| **Where they are** | Reels & Stories during gym/lunch, IG/TikTok in the evening when they're stewing about a lost deal, LinkedIn Tuesday mornings |
| **What they buy on** | Survival. Not productivity. They will pay $19 personal card if it might save the next deal. Will NOT submit a procurement form. |

### ICP-2 — "The Aspirational Top-Rep Hunter" (secondary, ~25%)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Title** | AE 2-4 years experience, mid-table on the leaderboard |
| **Company** | Same Series A–B SaaS profile, plus established mid-market (Salesloft / Outreach customer base) |

| **Pain trigger** | Watches the #1 rep on her team close back-to-back without breaking a sweat. Not in trouble. Just plateaued. Knows she's "almost there" but can't name the gap. |

### ICP-3 — "The Founder Selling Their Own Product" (tertiary, ~15%)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Solo founder / 2-person founding team, doing demos themselves |
| **Company** | Pre-seed to seed, $0–$1M ARR, no sales hire yet |

### ICP-4 — "The Sales Manager / Team Lead" (retarget only, NOT cold-prospect in v1)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Sales Manager, Head of Sales, VP Sales (small) |
| **Why retarget only** | Their buying motion is team-rollout, demo-call, procurement. That fights the "Try Free → tonvo.co" CTA. We let them self-discover via a rep on their team, then retarget with the Sales Team angle ($49/user). |

**Tradeoff acknowledged:** ICP-4 is the highest LTV segment. We are choosing to leave them in retargeting only because their buying motion does not match the PLG funnel we are testing. If v1 produces signups from this segment anyway, we'll add a manager-specific angle in v2.

## 2. Pain → Promise → Proof (per segment)

### ICP-1 — Quota-Slipping AE

- **Pain:** "I'm losing deals that should have closed. I can hear it after the call. I just can't hear it _during_ the call."
- **Promise:** Tonvo flags the buying signal and the objection in real time and tells you the line that closes. Your pitch doesn't change. Your close rate does.
- **Proof:** Ad11 shows the exact $50K deal you just lost — three signals you missed. Then the iPhone demo capture shows Tonvo catching all three in real time. Free for 3 sessions/month. No card.

### ICP-2 — Aspirational Top-Rep Hunter

- **Pain:** "The top rep on our team isn't smarter. She just _hears_ things I don't. I want that."
- **Promise:** Tonvo is the silent pattern-matcher the top rep has between her ears, running on every call. +27% close rate without changing your pitch.
- **Proof:** Ad10 chrome +27% with the "same pitch, same calls, same product — one thing changes" beat. Demo capture. Cohort line.

### ICP-3 — Founder

- **Pain:** "I built it, I love it, I can demo every feature — but my close rate is 9% and I don't know which 91% I'm doing wrong."
- **Promise:** Tonvo turns every demo into a coached close. You pitch the way you pitch. Tonvo handles the read.
- **Proof:** 30-second setup. Free tier. Works on Zoom/Meet/Teams. The same software your funded competitors will end up paying for.

## 3. Angles — 5 to test in v1

We need angles that survive HyperFrames production in a week. That means each one is either (a) a remix of Ad10/Ad11 with new copy and pacing, (b) a new scene stack on the existing visual primitives (chrome text, glass cards, iPhone 17 mockup, demo capture), or (c) a static + minimal motion direction. Nothing requiring on-camera actors, location shoots, or new SFX libraries.

### Angle A — "POV: You Just Lost $50K" (Ad11, already shipped)

- **Mechanic:** Second-person POV. Three signals you missed. Tonvo catches all three.
- **Targets:** ICP-1 primarily, ICP-2 secondarily.
- **Emotional register:** Loss aversion. The deal is already gone. This is the post-mortem.
- **Production status:** ✅ Done (1080×1920 9:16). Reformat to 1:1 for Feed placement.

### Angle B — "+27% Without Changing Your Pitch" (Ad10, already shipped)

- **Mechanic:** Big chrome +27% number, "same pitch / same calls / same product — one thing changes" beat, donut visualizing the cohort.
- **Targets:** ICP-2 primarily, ICP-1 as fallback.
- **Emotional register:** Edge. Curiosity. The number is the hook.
- **Production status:** ✅ Done (1080×1440 3:4). Reformat to 9:16 for Reels.

### Angle C — "The Gap" (NEW)

- **Mechanic:** Split screen. Left: "You" — close rate 23%, hesitates on the price objection, deal stalls. Right: "Top Rep" — close rate 41%, hears the buying signal, lands the close. Final beat: the only difference between them is what's running in their browser. Reveal Tonvo demo capture.
- **Targets:** ICP-2 primarily.
- **Why it's distinct:** B is about the lift. This is about the _person_ achieving the lift. Identity-driven instead of metric-driven.
- **Production:** Reuses chrome text, glass cards, demo capture. New scene composition, no new assets needed. Producable in 2-3 days.

### Angle D — "Stop Pitching From Memory" (NEW, renamed from "Stop Winging It")

- **Mechanic:** Cuts of an AE on a Zoom call, sweating. Voiceover: "You walked into your last 12 demos with a deck and a vibe. Your competition didn't." Reveal: Tonvo catches the objection, surfaces the line, deal closes. Hard cut to CTA.
- **Targets:** ICP-1, ICP-3.
- **Emotional register:** Slight shame, then permission. "It's okay. The pros use a copilot."
- **Production:** Stock-footage-driven OR all-on-canvas with no faces (chrome text + audio waveform + demo capture). I recommend the all-on-canvas version — faster to ship, on-brand, no licensing risk.

### Angle E — "The Silent Cofounder" (NEW)

- **Mechanic:** "Every solo founder needs a cofounder. Yours just runs in your browser." Frame Tonvo as the partner who handles the closing while the founder handles the pitch. Tight beat: founder asks → Tonvo flags → founder closes → revenue card pops.
- **Targets:** ICP-3 primarily.
- **Emotional register:** Aspirational, partnership-coded. Anti-loneliness pitch.
- **Production:** Reuses iPhone 17 mockup + demo capture. New copy + voiceover. 2 days.

### Angles deliberately considered and cut

- **"Manager scorecard / coach your reps"** — kills the PLG CTA. Saved for v2 / Sales Team angle if v1 succeeds.
- **"Comment-bait + finger-tap"** — cheap, but visually inconsistent with the silver-sweep / iPhone-17 aesthetic we've spent 2 weeks building. Not worth the brand drag for a marginal CTR bump.
- **"AI-coach influencer testimonial"** — we don't have one yet. Building one in 5 days is fake. Skip.

**Final cut for v1:** Ship A, B, C, D. Keep E in reserve as an iteration drop in week 2 if A or D underperforms.

## 4. Funnel Hypothesis — single test for v1

> **Hypothesis:** A US-based AE who has missed quota in the last 60 days will pay $19/mo on her personal card if a 30-second ad makes her _hear_ the deal she lost yesterday and proves Tonvo would have caught it. The friction is not awareness — it's belief that the demo is real and the setup is genuinely 30 seconds.

### Funnel mechanic

```
Cold Reel/Feed impression (US, 25–45, sales-adjacent interests)
      ↓ ~1.0–2.5% CTR target
Click → tonvo.co/login (with utm tagging)
      ↓ ~25–40% landing→signup target (free tier, no card)
Signup event (primary KPI)
      ↓ ~30–50% activate (first session within 7 days)
Activated user
      ↓ ~6–10% free→paid in 30 days (industry-average PLG)
Closer ($19) or Sales Team ($49) paid conversion
```

### Where we expect friction (in order)

1. **Hold/scroll-stop.** First 1.5s of every ad has to land. Ad11's "$50K POV" hook tested well in dry runs. Ad10's "+27%" chrome number is a softer hook. Angle C and D need visceral first frames.
2. **Belief that the demo is real.** "AI-says-this-on-your-call" is in the GPT-wrapper bucket of consumer skepticism. Mitigation: we lead with the actual product UI capture (`assets/demo-session.mp4`), not a stylized illustration.
3. **The setup-is-30-seconds claim.** If they click and the landing makes them install a Chrome extension and grant 4 OAuth scopes, we lose them. Dependency: Emilien confirms tonvo.co/login flow is genuinely <60s to first session. (Open question in brief.)
4. **Free→paid friction.** Out of scope for v1 ad creative. v1 is a CPS test, not a paid-conversion test. We measure free→paid as a leading indicator, not a target.

### What single thing v1 is testing

**Whether the loss-aversion / missed-money angle (A + D) outperforms the aspirational lift angle (B + C) for cold US AEs at the signup event.**

If A+D wins → v2 leans further into deal-loss reenactments and rep-anxiety territory.
If B+C wins → v2 leans into top-rep identity and metric-driven narratives.
If they're tied within ±15% CPS → we have a portfolio, not a winner, and we add E and split-test against more specific titles.

## 5. KPI Targets

These are honest ranges for cold US traffic on Meta in mid-2026 for a B2B SaaS PLG offer with no brand recognition. Not promises.

| Metric | Pessimistic | Target | Stretch | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CPM** (US, sales/SMB interests) | $35 | $22–$28 | $18 | US B2B is expensive. Reels cheaper than Feed. |
| **CTR (link)** | 0.6% | 1.2–1.8% | 2.5% | Ad11 hook strength suggests upper end is reachable for the strongest angle, but cold avg will be in the band. |
| **CPC** | $4.50 | $1.80–$2.50 | $1.20 | Function of CPM × CTR. |
| **Landing → Signup conversion** | 18% | 28–35% | 45% | Depends almost entirely on tonvo.co/login flow being clean. Pixel + CAPI must be firing. |
| **CPS (primary KPI)** | $25 | $8–$15 | $5 | This is the number we optimize on. Anything sub-$15 in week 1 is a green light to scale. |
| **Free → Activated (1st session)** | 25% | 35–45% | 55% | Activation = 1 live session within 7 days of signup. |
| **Free → Paid (30 days)** | 4% | 6–9% | 12% | Out of scope for v1; tracked as leading indicator. |
| **Hold rate (3s, video)** | 35% | 50–60% | 70% | Ad11/Ad10 cinematic pacing should over-index here. If we don't hit 50%, hooks are wrong. |

### Honesty about what we don't know

- **CPM is volatile.** $22–$28 is an _educated_ band based on US B2B benchmarks; we may discover Tonvo's specific audience is $40+ if Meta classifies us in the saturated "AI tools" interest bucket. We adapt at 72h.
- **The +27% claim** is on landing and on Ad10. If Meta's automated review flags it, we have a backup creative without the explicit number.
- **The signup CPS target ($8–$15)** assumes the Pixel + CAPI is firing correctly with a properly deduplicated `Signup` event. If that's not in place at launch (open question 2 in brief), CPS reads will be unreliable for the first 48–72h until Meta's algo learns the event.

## 6. Anti-Strategy — what we will explicitly NOT do

### 1. No "Book a Demo" CTA. Anywhere.

The whole product economics depend on PLG. "Book a Demo" routes a $19/mo prospect into a sales process that costs more than the LTV. Every ad CTA is `Try Free → tonvo.co`. Manager-team buyers self-discover later via reps; we do not chase them in cold prospecting.

### 2. No feature-list ads, no "AI-powered" hype.

We do not say "AI-powered closing platform with intelligent objection handling and conversational analytics." We say what the software does on a call. Mechanism over buzzword. If we cannot describe the feature as a beat in a 30-second ad, it's not in the ad.

### 3. No LinkedIn-style productivity bait or generic "10x your sales" content.

That entire genre has a CPS floor of ~$40 because the audience has been trained to scroll past it. We are visually distinct (silver-sweep chrome, glass cards, iPhone-17 mockup, real product UI capture) and copy-distinct (loss aversion, specific dollar amounts, real product behavior). Anything that could be re-skinned as a generic "AI for X" ad gets cut.

### Bonus: things we will also not do

- **No new visual languages in v1.** We use the chrome / glass / iPhone-17 system we already shipped. Saves 4 days of brand drift.
- **No paid influencer in v1.** No relationship, no time, no budget. Earned creator content is a v3 problem.
- **No live filming of Emilien in v1.** His time is better spent on product and approving creative than on shoots/retakes. _Updated v1.2:_ AI-avatar paths (Hedra Character-3 from a still photo + voice clone) are NOT live filming and are permitted when tech-director justifies the founder register — currently scoped to Brief E S1 only.
