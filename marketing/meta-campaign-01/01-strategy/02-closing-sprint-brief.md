## Why this sprint

V1 strategy (01-strategy.md) ships 5 angles around a generic +27% / lost-deal narrative. We are now opening a **focused sprint on THE CLOSE itself** — not pipeline, not discovery, not demo. The exact 90-second window where the rep has to **ask for the deal** and the buyer either says yes, objects, or stalls.

This is where 60% of B2B deals die. It is the highest-emotion moment in the call. It is also the moment where Tonvo's real-time copilot creates the most visible delta vs. "winging it." We want creative that lives entirely in that moment.

## Scope guardrails (do NOT drift)

1. **Subject = the close moment only.** Not prospecting. Not discovery. Not demo. Not onboarding. The 60–120 seconds when the rep is about to ask for the order or the buyer is about to object.
2. **Visual language stays locked** — chrome/silver-sweep text, glass cards, iPhone 17 mockup, real product UI capture (`assets/demo-session.mp4`). No new aesthetic.
3. **CTA stays locked** — `Try Free → tonvo.co`. No "Book a demo." No "Talk to sales."
4. **Faceless mandate (Lane A)** — chrome/text/glass only for this sprint's outputs. UGC Lane B is parallel work, not this sprint.
5. **No Cluely call-outs in this sprint** — the comparison static already exists. Closing sprint stays on the moment, not on competitors.
6. **Number-driven copy** — every angle hook must contain either a $ amount, a %, or a count. "the line that closes" is good; "better closing" is dead.

## Target ICP for this sprint

- **Primary (70%):** ICP-1 — "The Quota-Slipping AE" who has lost a deal at the close in the last 30 days
- **Secondary (30%):** ICP-2 — "The Aspirational Top-Rep Hunter" who notices the #1 rep closes 41% while she closes 23%

ICP-3 (founder) and ICP-4 (manager) are NOT targets for this sprint — different psychology around closing.

## Hard constraints on closing angles

The brief is "closing." That word can mean many things. Lock the team to one of these 6 close-moment subjects per angle — no fuzzy "general sales" angles allowed:

1. **The unasked close** — rep talked for 47 min, never asked. Tonvo flags the moment to ask.
2. **The killer objection** — "send me the pricing" / "I need to think" / "let me check with my team" — Tonvo surfaces the rebuttal that lands the close in the next 30 sec.
3. **The buying signal** — "how soon could we start?" / "what does onboarding look like?" — Tonvo flags it as a close-now signal that 70% of reps miss.
4. **The silence after the ask** — rep finally asks, buyer goes silent, rep talks past the close. Tonvo coaches: hold the silence, count to 7.
5. **The mirror close** — buyer says "it's a lot of money." Tonvo surfaces the exact mirror line that flips it.
6. **The 7-second close** — the entire close happens in 7 seconds at the right moment. Tonvo signals that moment.

Each angle must pick ONE of these 6 subjects and go deep. No angle covers two.

## Production budget

- 6 ads × (9:16 master + 1:1 reformat) = 12 deliverables
- Reuse existing HF primitives. No new SFX. No new music. No new actors.
- 1 ad = max 2 production days at HF defaults.
- Whole sprint shippable in 6–8 days end-to-end if angles, copy, briefs land in next 24h.

## What kills an angle

- "Better closing rate" with no specific %, $, or count → KILL
- Any visual that breaks the chrome/glass/iPhone-17 stack → KILL
- Any CTA other than `Try Free → tonvo.co` → KILL
- Any face on screen in Lane A → KILL (Lane A faceless mandate)
- Anything that requires a feature Tonvo doesn't ship today → KILL
