## Why the ranking

- **#1 Count to 7** ranks first because the subject (silence after the ask) is the highest-frequency rep failure in ICP-1's last 30 days and the cheapest to dramatize with our locked stack (timer + waveform + one Tonvo card). Highest CPS confidence.
- **#2 Line That Closes** owns the most-searched rep pain ("how do I handle 'I need to think'"). Demo capture does the heavy lifting; copy carries low invention risk.
- **#3 Signal You Missed** is the ICP-2 pride play and the cleanest counterpoint to #1's shame — we want both registers live to AB-test ICP-1 shame vs ICP-2 pride at the signup event.
- **#4 You Never Asked** is the highest-pain hook on paper (`47 minutes. 8,412 words. 0 asks.`) but slightly lower confidence because the failure mode (didn't ask at all) is more shameful than #1 (asked, then talked past) — some ICP-1 viewers will deflect.
- **#5 Mirror** and **#6 7-Second** are reserve because: #5 leans on copywriter delivering a tactic-grade rebuttal line correctly on first pass (craft risk), and #6 stacks 3 mini-stories in 30s (pacing risk inside HF defaults).
