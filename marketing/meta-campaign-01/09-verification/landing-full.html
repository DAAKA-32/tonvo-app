Questions closers ask.

Everything you need to know before you raise your close rate with Tonvo.

Is Tonvo a tool or a growth system?

Tonvo is a Growth-as-a-Service system — we sell closing rate, not software. You plug in, and the outcome we deliver is measurable: more deals closed, bigger tickets, fewer objections lost. The app is just the delivery mechanism.

How fast does the close rate actually move?

Most closers on Tonvo see a measurable close-rate lift inside 14 days — not because they changed their pitch, but because they stopped missing the moments that decide a deal. The average uplift across 30 days is +27% close rate.

What exactly do I get during a call?

The exact line to say when it matters: the objection-handler, the price anchor, the commitment ask, the reframe. No dashboards to read live — just the next move, surfaced the instant the signal hits.

Do I need to change my sales process or pitch?

No. Tonvo layers on top of your existing process. Your pitch, your deck, your offer — unchanged. What changes is the rate at which they end in a signed deal.

Can my prospect see anything?

Never. Tonvo runs in a separate window, invisible to the other side — even on screen share. Your edge stays your edge.

Is there a guarantee on the outcome?

Yes. If your close rate doesn't move in 7 days, we refund you. We sell results — if you don't get them, we don't keep your money.
