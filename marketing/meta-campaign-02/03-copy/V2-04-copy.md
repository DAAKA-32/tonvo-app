## Notes to creative-director

**Must be visual (non-negotiable):**
1. **1:1 aspect ratio, not 9:16.** This is the Feed placement variant per brief §7. The framing changes the visual logic — the Zoom thumbnail can sit upper-half with overlays lower-half rather than the 9:16 stack. Don't auto-crop V2-03; rebuild the comp for 1:1.
2. **The split-frame at 00:11–00:14 is the entire payoff of the ad.** Left side grayscale + red "FOLLOW UP" = the viewer's last week. Right side full color + chrome `CLOSED` = the alternate timeline with Tonvo. The contrast must be sharp; if the grayscale is too subtle the A/B reads as one thing happening twice instead of two outcomes side-by-side. Aim for 30% saturation drop on left, 100% on right.
3. **The typewriter line at 00:14–00:14.5 must match the landing verbatim.** "Without missing a signal." Tighter time slot than V2-03 (0.5s vs 1.0s) so type-in cadence is faster, but the font, monospace style, and cursor blink must still match the landing's. Continuity claim in brief §5 only holds if typography matches.
4. **iPhone 17 mockup at 00:07–00:11** carries the product reveal in 4 seconds. Tighter than V2-03's full UI showcase; this one's a single glass card pulse over the mockup edge. The compactness is intentional for Feed scroll speed.

**Can be audio-only (lower production lift):**
- The buyer audio at 00:03–00:07 is a single synthesized line `[dramatization — synthesized buyer line, not a real call clip]` per v1 Resolution-1. Single line is cheaper than V2-03's three lines.
- The AE response "sure, I'll follow up." can be VO or text-only depending on production timeline. If VO, it's the AE narrator voice (Ava) — not a different actor. Same speaker performs her own past line.

**What this ad must NOT do:**
- No retrospective POV that strays into pure post-mortem. The "POV" frame is the on-ramp; the live capture is the proof. If the cut leans too heavily on "remember last week," it becomes Angle A (v1 POV-$50K). Hold the line on present-tense product capture in the middle third.
- No "AI" / "intelligent" / "smart" / "powerful" / "next-gen" anywhere — banned.
- No percentage stat anywhere in the cut.
- No founder-on-camera.

## Variants A/B — alt-hooks for rotation test

If V2-04 ships and hook rate underperforms (<35% at 3s) within first 48h, swap the hook beat at 00:00–00:03 for one of:

**Alt-A (specific-number cold-open, harder edge):**
**"Last call: 1 signal. 0 catches. 0 deals."**
- 8 words. Stacks three flat numbers in descending order; the rhythm mimics a stopwatch losing seconds. More visceral than the POV frame for ICP-2 viewers who respond to measurement rigor over self-recognition. Numbers are illustrative — ship with `[dramatization — illustrative call stats]` footer per v1 Resolution-1.
- Visual: same Zoom thumbnail fade-up at 1.0s. Caption is the three-stat stack in monospace, comma-replaced with mid-dots for chrome rhythm.

**Alt-B (contradiction, swipe-file lift from v1 Angle B):**
**"Same call. Same words. One catch different."**
- 7 words. Pure contradiction archetype. Sets up the split-frame payoff at 00:11–00:14 before the viewer gets there — the hook tells them what they're about to see, then the ad delivers it. Works as both a comprehension scaffold (low-attention viewers) and a payoff confirmation (high-attention viewers).
- Visual: black bg first 0.6s, then a single Zoom thumbnail fades up. No glass card on the hook beat itself — saves it for the body. Cleaner top of the ad than V2-04's default.
