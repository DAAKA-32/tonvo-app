## Hook (3-second scroll-stop)

**Spoken / overlay:** **"Three signals. Thirty seconds. Watch."**

- 5 words. Specific-number cold-open + invitation archetype. The third word ("Watch.") is a direct command — it earns the viewer's attention by promising what's about to happen, then delivers it inside the same ad.
- Visual: 0.0–0.3s — black bg, chrome wordmark `3 · 30 · WATCH` builds left-to-right with sub-bass under each numeral. 0.3–1.0s — wordmark holds. 1.0–1.5s — wordmark dims to 20% opacity and pulls back; a live Zoom-style call thumbnail fades up underneath at 50% scale, lower-third. The transition signals "the show is about to start."
- Audio: 0.0–0.3s — three discrete chrome ticks (one per numeral). 0.3–1.0s — single sustained ambient pad. 1.0s — pad cuts to room-tone leakage from the Zoom thumbnail (synthesized buyer murmuring, low). This audio shift cues "we're now inside the call."

## Notes to creative-director

**Must be visual (non-negotiable):**
1. **The Zoom call thumbnail must read as live, not as a screenshot.** This is the entire premise — "watch this happen in real time." Use the existing `assets/demo-session.mp4` if possible; if not, build a 22s synthesized session with a single neutral-pose buyer loop and three timed buyer audio drops. The thumbnail framing matters more than the buyer's face — keep it lower-third, slightly blurred, more "we're observing" than "we're starring." If the thumbnail reads static, the whole ad collapses.
2. **The three glass card flips must each land on-beat with their respective buyer audio cues.** Card 1 flip at 00:07, card 2 flip at 00:12, card 3 flip at 00:17 — each ~1s after the synthesized buyer line ends. The visual rhythm IS the demonstration that Tonvo is keeping up. If the cards lag the audio, the product looks slow; if they precede the audio, the product looks scripted. Tight sync mandatory.
3. **The typewriter line at 00:20–00:21 must match the landing verbatim.** "Without missing a signal." Same font, same monospace, same cursor cadence as the landing's typewriter loop. The landing→ad continuity in brief §5 is the entire V2 hypothesis — it only holds if the typography literally matches.
4. **iPhone 17 mockup at 00:18–00:20** for the Tonvo UI stack reveal. Per the memory rule.

**Can be audio-only (lower production lift):**
- The buyer audio at 00:03–00:18 is three synthesized lines `[dramatization — synthesized buyer lines, not real call clips]` per v1 Resolution-1. ElevenLabs or EdgeTTS placeholder is fine; tag the asset accordingly in the production file.
- The chrome `SIGNAL 1/2/3` stamps top-right are nice-to-have. The card flips carry the load. If timeline is tight, drop the corner stamps.

**What this ad must NOT do:**
- No retrospective POV ("here's what you missed last week"). This ad is live-action; the whole point is that the proof is happening on-screen as the viewer watches. Brief §3 names this as the distinction from v1's POV-$50K post-mortem. Hold the line on present-tense.
- No "AI" / "intelligent" / "smart" / "powerful" anywhere — banned.
- No percentage stat anywhere in the cut. The three signals on-screen ARE the proof.
- No founder-on-camera.

## Variants A/B — alt-hooks for rotation test

**Alt-A (direct address, accusation register):**
**"You missed three buying signals on her last call."**
- 8 words. Pulls v1 Angle A Hook 3 forward into V2 ("You missed three signals on that call.") but specifies "buying signals" to align with Angle G's spine. Slightly harder edge than H3 — lands on the AE who already half-knows she fumbled. Visual stays the same Zoom thumbnail fade-up at 1.0s; only the caption changes.
- Why it might pull harder: the v1 hook variant tested well in copywriter discipline review (`hooks-refined.md` Angle A Hook 3) and the "three signals" specificity already proved in v1's discipline pass. Swap is zero production cost — just a copy change.

**Alt-B (visual-first, near-wordless):**
**[no copy] — chrome counter ticks `01 · 02 · 03` over a Zoom thumbnail, each tick lands a green pip on the call.**
- Wordless variant for sound-off feed coverage (~60% of Reels). Sub-bass on each tick. Counter freezes at 3 with all pips lit. Caption stamps in at 1.3s: `LIVE.`
- Why it might pull harder: every other V2 ad has spoken/written hooks. A wordless variant in the rotation covers the audience-segment we'd otherwise miss. The pip pattern is the same swipe-file mechanic v1 used for Angle A Hook 4 (proven discipline-pass primitive).
