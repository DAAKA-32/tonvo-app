## Hook (3-second scroll-stop)

**Spoken / overlay:** **"Stop typing. Start closing."**

- 4 words. Outrage / category-rename archetype (same register as v1 Ad-D "Stop pitching from memory"). The two imperatives are paired so the second one frames the first as the actual ask.
- Visual: 0.0–0.2s — black fades up. 0.2s — full line lands in white serif (deviation from chrome — editorial register, intentional). Center, dead-center, no animation other than the fade. 0.2–3.0s — frozen. The stillness IS the hook in a feed of motion.
- Audio: 0.0–0.2s — single sub-bass impact on the fade-in. 0.2–3.0s — total silence. No riser, no music bed. The dead air is the scroll-stop.

## Notes to creative-director

**Must be visual (non-negotiable):**
1. **3 seconds of dead-still serif at 00:00–00:03.** This is the entire mechanic. Every other ad in the feed will have motion in the first 1.5s. V2-02's edge is the stillness. Do not add a subtle parallax, do not add a slow zoom, do not add a fade-up animation past 0.2s. Frozen. The 1.3s of dead air is uncomfortable on a phone scroll — which is exactly the point.
2. **The typewriter line at 00:09–00:11 must match the landing verbatim.** Same as V2-01: "Without taking a single note." Same font, same cursor cadence. Continuity claim in brief §5 requires it.
3. **iPhone 17 mockup at 00:06–00:09.** Per the memory rule: all Tonvo product UI ships inside the IPhone17Mockup component.

**Can be audio-only (lower production lift):**
- The "STENOGRAPHER" strikethrough at 00:05 could ship as a static overlay without the sweep animation in v1. Sweep is polish; the strikethrough character is enough to land the gag.
- The transcript-scrolling-in-background at 00:06–00:09 can be a single static frame from a real Tonvo session if motion capture is too expensive to set up. The point is "the product is doing the typing now."

**What this ad must NOT do:**
- No music. The serif-on-black + silence is the entire scroll-stop primitive. Music kills it.
- No "AI" / "intelligent" / "smart" anywhere — banned.
- No founder-on-camera.
- No percentage stat anywhere in the cut.

## Variants A/B — alt-hooks for rotation test

If V2-02 ships and hold rate underperforms (<25% at 8s) within first 48h, swap the hook beat at 00:00–00:03 for one of:

**Alt-A (specific-number cold-open):**
**"Last call. 8,400 words typed. Zero closed."**
- 7 words. Stacks the swipe-file specific-number mechanic onto the offload angle. The 8,400 number reads as measured (not round, not editorial). Echoes v1 closing-sprint angle #4's 8,412-word frame but reframes from "you didn't ask" to "you couldn't ask because you were typing." Number is illustrative-not-verifiable so it ships as a "[dramatization — illustrative call stats]" footer like v1 Resolution-1.

**Alt-B (direct accusation, harder edge):**
**"Your notes won't close it. You will."**
- 7 words. Same angle, accusation rather than imperative. Pulls the AE viewer who deflects "Stop typing." as preachy. Validates the work she did (the notes exist) before naming the missing piece (closing). Slightly cocky register lands without being shame-coded.
- Visual: same serif on black, same 3s freeze. Only the copy changes — production cost is zero.
